How the partnership works
01

We review the opportunity

We learn about your channel, business, audience, customers, geography, content and existing monetization. Together, we identify how Plant This could provide real value to your community.

02

We design the pilot

We prepare a focused 90-day collaboration that may include:

  • A dedicated landing page
  • A web-to-app funnel
  • Partner attribution
  • A campaign link, QR code or offer code
  • A subscription offer
  • A curated care program or plant collection
  • Selected content or recommendations
  • Campaign analytics

Standard pilot infrastructure is developed and managed by KissMyApps. Features requiring substantial custom development are discussed and approved separately.

03

We prepare the campaign

We help structure the message, funnel and promotional materials. Depending on the collaboration, this may include:

  • Campaign positioning
  • Landing-page copy
  • App demonstration flow
  • Visual campaign assets
  • QR codes and tracking links
  • Promotional calls to action
  • Partner-specific subscription offers
  • Seasonal content concepts
  • Nursery or in-store materials
04

You introduce Plant This

You promote the app through the channels that already work for your audience:

  • YouTube video integrations
  • Plant identification demonstrations

  • Website placements
  • Workshops and plant events

  • Care cards and purchase inserts

Authentic, recurring promotion generally creates better results than a single sponsored mention.

05

We track the complete journey

We measure:

  • Landing-page visits and funnel engagement
  • App Store visits and app installs
  • Offer-code redemptions
  • Trial starts and paid subscriptions
  • Renewals and refunds
  • Attributable net revenue
06

You receive the agreed percentage of net revenue generated by customers attributed to your partnership. Successful pilots may expand into premium care programs, seasonal campaigns, creator collections, nursery integrations, product recommendations or deeper product collaboration.

Example creator collaboration

"Seven Days to Healthier Houseplants"

A plant creator publishes a video introducing a simple seven-day plant-care challenge. During the video, the creator demonstrates Plant This and invites viewers to join through a dedicated campaign link.

The campaign journey:

  1. The viewer opens the creator's Plant This landing page.
  2. The page introduces the challenge and partner offer.
  3. The viewer installs Plant This.
  4. The app opens with partner-specific onboarding.
  5. The viewer identifies or adds their plants.
  6. The app creates care tasks and reminders.
  7. The viewer starts a premium subscription.
  8. The subscription is attributed to the partner.

The collaboration could later expand into:

Seasonal care programs · Premium plant guides · Creator-curated plant collections · Plant-health challenges · Educational videos · Product recommendations · Live workshops · Exclusive partner content

Plant This provides the product and conversion infrastructure. You provide the expertise and audience.

Example nursery collaboration

Nursery-branded plant collections · Seasonal campaigns · In-store workshops · Product recommendations · Loyalty offers · Local plant availability · Post-purchase care programs
