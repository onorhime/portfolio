How the partnership works
01

We review the opportunity

We learn about your channel, business, audience, geography, content and existing monetization. Together, we identify how Coin Flip could provide real value to your community.

02

We design the pilot

We prepare a focused 90-day collaboration that may include:

  • A dedicated landing page
  • A web-to-app funnel
  • Partner attribution
  • A campaign link or offer code
  • A subscription offer
  • Selected content or recommendations
  • Campaign analytics

Standard pilot infrastructure is developed and managed by KissMyApps. Features requiring substantial custom development are discussed and approved separately.

03

We prepare the campaign

We help structure the message, funnel and promotional materials. Depending on the collaboration, this may include:

  • Campaign positioning
  • Landing-page copy
  • App demonstration flow
  • Visual campaign assets
  • QR codes and tracking links
  • Promotional calls to action
  • Partner-specific subscription offers
04

You introduce Coin Flip

You promote the app through the channels that already work for your audience:

  • YouTube video integrations
  • Dedicated app demonstrations
  • Video descriptions and pinned comments
  • Newsletters and community posts
  • Social media content
  • Website placements
  • Coin-show demonstrations
  • In-store or event QR codes

Authentic, recurring promotion generally creates better results than a single sponsored mention.

05

We track the complete journey

We measure:

  • Landing-page visits and funnel engagement
  • App Store visits and app installs
  • Offer-code redemptions
  • Trial starts and paid subscriptions
  • Renewals and refunds
  • Attributable net revenue
06

We share and grow

You receive the agreed percentage of net revenue generated by customers attributed to your partnership. Successful pilots may expand into premium content, creator collections, dealer integrations, exclusive programs or deeper product collaboration.

Example collaboration

"Five Coins to Check Before You Spend Them"

A coin creator publishes a video featuring five commonly overlooked coins. During the video, the creator demonstrates Coin Flip and invites viewers to scan their own coins through a dedicated campaign link.

The campaign journey:

  1. The viewer opens the creator's Coin Flip landing page.
  2. The page introduces the app and partner offer.
  3. The viewer installs Coin Flip.
  4. The app opens with partner-specific onboarding.
  5. The viewer scans and saves coins.
  6. The viewer starts a premium subscription.
  7. The subscription is attributed to the partner.

The collaboration could later expand into:

Monthly coin-discovery challenges · Premium collecting guides · Featured coin collections · Educational videos · Recommendations for new collectors · Dealer or auction integrations · Seasonal campaigns · Exclusive partner offers

Coin Flip provides the product and conversion infrastructure. You provide the authority and audience.

What makes a strong partner

We are looking for partners who:

  • Have a relevant and engaged coin audience
  • Publish or communicate consistently
  • Can demonstrate the app authentically
  • Are willing to promote the partnership more than once
  • Provide responsible information about coin identification and value
  • Want recurring revenue rather than a one-time sponsorship

  • Are open to reviewing results and improving the campaign
